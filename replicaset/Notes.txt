# ReplicaSet

- ye pod ka naam , apne naam k jaisa hi rakhta hai, par alag alag id k sath 

- Ye template me diye gye pods ka replica bnata hai 

- iske spec me tin parameters required hoti hai replicas, seletor, template 

- Advantage : Auto healing capability of pods (recreation)
- Disadvantage : No rolling update, causes slight downtime
